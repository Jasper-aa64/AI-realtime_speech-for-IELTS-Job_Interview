## Part 1: Real Questions by Topic

### Topic 1: Home / Accommodation

**Season**: 2026 (confirmed across multiple sources: IELTSLiz, IELTSAdvantage, Blogspot 2025/2026, ReadingIELTS)

Real questions reported/confirmed for 2025-2026:

1. Do you live in a house or a flat?
2. Who do you live with? / Do you live alone or with other people?
3. What is your favourite room in your home?
4. What do you like most about your home?
5. What changes would you like to make to your home (or your room)?
6. How are the walls decorated in your home?
7. Do you plan to continue living there in the future?
8. What facilities are there near your home?
9. What is your neighbourhood like? / Do you like your neighbourhood?
10. Is your neighbourhood a good place to live?
11. Do you think the environment around your home affects your mood?
12. Would you like to move to another place in the future?
13. What kind of people live in your area?
14. Are there many rooms in your home?
15. Do most people live in houses in your country?

**Recommended 6-7 for JSON (most frequently reported)**:
- Do you live in a house or a flat?
- Who do you live with?
- What is your favourite room in your home?
- What do you like most about your home?
- What changes would you like to make to your home?
- Is your neighbourhood a good place to live?
- Do you plan to continue living there in the future?

### Topic 3: Technology

**Season**: 2026 (confirmed: IELTSLiz 2026, Blogspot 2025/2026, IELTSAdvantage, Cathoven Part 3 themes)

Note: In IELTS, "Technology" Part 1 usually comes under Internet, Computers, or Mobile Phones headings. The blogspot 2025/2026 page directly reports technology questions as real test questions.

Real questions reported/confirmed for 2025-2026:

1. Do you often use a computer or laptop for your studies or work?
2. Do you find typing easy or difficult?

4. Do you prefer typing on a keyboard or using a touchscreen?
5. Has technology changed the way you study or work?
6. Do you use technology to help organise your daily life?
7. How do you usually get online?
8. How important is the Internet to you?
9. Do you use the Internet more for work or in your free time?

12. Do you think you use the Internet too much?
13. Will people continue to use computers in the future?
14. Do you have a mobile phone?

**Recommended 6-7 for JSON**:
- Do you often use a computer or laptop for your studies or work?

- Do you find typing easy or difficult?

**Cue Card 1** — NEW (Jan-Apr / May-Aug 2026, Cathoven confirmed)

> **Describe a time when you felt proud of a family member**
>
> - When it happened
> - Who the person is
> - What the person did
> - And explain why you felt proud of him/her
>
> **Rounding**: And explain why you felt proud of him/her.
>
> **P3 Theme**: Family pride, children's development, rewards & achievement
>
> Sample P3 questions: When would parents feel proud of their children? Should parents reward children? Is it good to reward children too often?

## Caveats / Notes

1. **IELTS does not officially publish exam questions**. All questions listed here come from candidate-reported sources, reputable IELTS preparation websites, and community-sourced databases that track real exam appearances.

2. **Cathoven's May-Aug 2026 bank** (55 cue cards, published/updated April 2026) is the most current source available for the May-Aug 2026 season and explicitly distinguishes NEW vs RETAINED topics.

3. **IELTSFever Jan-Apr 2026 list** is based on weekly community updates of real exam questions reported by test-takers; it had 36 topics confirmed as of the scrape date.

4. **Part 1 "Technology" topic**: IELTS rarely labels a topic simply "Technology" — it appears as Internet, Computers, Mobile Phones, or Apps. Questions about typing (keyboard/touchscreen) were directly reported as 2025/2026 real questions from the blogspot source.

5. **Season overlap**: Jan-Apr 2026 and May-Aug 2026 share many "retained" topics (roughly 28 of 55 per Cathoven). The 15 cue cards listed above are drawn from both seasons to provide the most current real questions.

6. **Part 1 question counts**: Each topic above provides 15+ real questions to choose from. The JSON files currently use 6 questions each — the "Recommended" sets above prioritize the most frequently reported questions across all sources.
